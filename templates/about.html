About VERITAS

A simple way to take a closer look at news before you believe it or share it.

What is VERITAS?

VERITAS is an AI-powered news checking tool. It reads news text and gives a prediction about whether the writing looks more like real or false news. You can also use its fact-checking feature to look for information from other sources.

How to use VERITAS

1
Enter the news

Paste the news text or a link to an article.

2
Read the result

VERITAS shows what the AI thinks, using a clear label and color.

3
Check other sources

When available, use the fact-checking section to investigate a specific statement.

4
Decide carefully

Read reliable sources before sharing important news.

What do the colors mean?

🟢
Green — Possibly real

The AI leans towards the news being real. This is not proof that it is true.

🔴
Red — Possibly false

The AI leans towards the news being false. You should check the claim before trusting the result.

🟡
Yellow — Not sure

The AI cannot give a clear prediction. More checking is needed.

Important: VERITAS can make mistakes

Do not treat the AI result as a final fact-check. A high score does not mean a news story is definitely true or false. The scores describe the AI's prediction, not the real-world chance that the story is true. A page found during an online search is also not automatically proof.

For important claims, compare reporting from trusted news organisations, check dates and original sources, and read any published fact-check carefully.
